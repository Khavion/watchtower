# What Khavion delivers

Buyer language only. Each line says what the client actually receives, not the
technology used to produce it. Drafters may only claim what is on this list;
`boundaries.md` says what is off it.

## Cloud cost optimization & Kubernetes autoscaling
You receive a line-item map of where your AWS bill actually goes, the ten
changes that cut it, and the autoscaling configuration that keeps it cut -
typically 20–70% reduction on compute-heavy workloads.

## RAG and LLM application delivery
You receive a working retrieval-augmented application on your data, in your
cloud, with evaluation numbers that tell you whether it is good enough to ship
- not a demo, a deployable.

## GenAI readiness assessment
You receive a ranked list of the AI use cases in your business worth building,
what each costs to run at your volume, and the data/security gaps to close
first: a build decision you can defend to your board.

## AI agents
You receive an agent that completes a defined business workflow end to end
(intake, triage, drafting, data entry) with human checkpoints where money or
customers are touched, and logs you can audit.

## Amazon Bedrock & Azure OpenAI implementation
You receive production model access wired into your VPC with guardrails, cost
controls, and observability: the setup your security team approves, not the
one that ships an API key in a Lambda.

## LLMOps
You receive the pipeline around your models: evaluation on every change,
version-pinned prompts, spend tracking per feature, and alerts before quality
or cost drifts.

## Architecture review
You receive a written assessment of your cloud/AI architecture against what it
needs to do next year: the three risks that will bite first and the sequence of
fixes, sized in calendar weeks.
