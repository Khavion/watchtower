# Proposal

Starter template, drafted 2026-07-25. This is the document a buyer reads before
they agree; `templates/sow.md` is what they sign afterwards. It is short on
purpose: a solo consultant's proposal wins on being specific and easy to say yes
to, not on length.

The proposal writer fills the `{{ }}` fields from a real conversation and never
invents any of them. Anything it could not learn from the call is left as a
question rather than a guess.
