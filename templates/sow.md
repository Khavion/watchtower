# Statement of Work

Starter template, drafted 2026-07-25 from `brain/offers.md`,
`brain/scope-guardrails.md` and the payment terms Zohaib confirmed. The proposal
writer fills the `{{ }}` fields from a real deal and leaves everything else
alone.

**This is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** It is a
working commercial document that covers the things that cause disputes in solo
consulting: what is in scope, what is not, what "done" means, when money moves,
and who owns the output. Before the first engagement of any size, it is worth
one hour of a Texas contracts lawyer's time.

## 8. Intellectual property

On receipt of payment in full, Client owns the deliverables produced
specifically for Client under this SOW.

Consultant retains ownership of pre-existing materials, methods, tooling,
templates and general knowledge, including anything developed before this SOW or
independently of it, and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use
those materials to the extent they are embedded in the deliverables.

Nothing in this SOW transfers rights in third-party or open-source components,
which remain governed by their own licences.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's confidential information with at least
reasonable care, use it only to perform this SOW, and not disclose it without
written permission. This survives two years past termination.

Consultant will not name Client, quote Client metrics, or describe Client's
systems publicly without Client's prior written permission. This is Consultant's
standing practice, not merely a contractual term.

## 10. Warranties and limitation of liability

Consultant will perform the services in a professional and workmanlike manner
consistent with industry standards, and warrants that the deliverables will meet
the acceptance criteria in section 4.

Except for that warranty, the deliverables are provided as is. Consultant does
not warrant any specific cost saving, business result, revenue outcome, or model
accuracy figure. Estimated savings ranges discussed before or during the
engagement are estimates, not commitments, and are not terms of this SOW.

**Consultant's total liability under this SOW is limited to the fees actually
paid by Client under it.** Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental,
special, or consequential damages, or for lost profits.

## 11. Independent contractor

Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of
Client. Consultant controls the manner and means of performance, provides their
own equipment, and is responsible for their own taxes, insurance and benefits.
Nothing here creates an exclusive relationship in either direction.

## 12. Term and termination

This SOW runs until the deliverables are accepted or it is terminated.

Either party may terminate on fifteen days written notice. On termination,
Client pays for work completed to that date, and the deposit is non-refundable
once work has begun. Consultant will hand over work in progress in usable form.

## 13. Non-solicitation

Neither party will solicit the other's employees or contractors during this SOW
and for twelve months after, except through general public advertising.

## 14. Governing law

This SOW is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Disputes are subject to
the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts sitting in Harris
County, Texas. The parties will attempt good-faith resolution before filing.

## 15. Entire agreement

This SOW, together with any signed change orders, is the entire agreement on
this subject and supersedes prior discussions, proposals and correspondence.
Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is
unenforceable, the rest stays in force.
